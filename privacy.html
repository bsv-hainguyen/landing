Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aether (“Aether”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website or use the Aether application, including when you sign in with your Google account.

1. Information we collect

  • Google account information. When you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google shares with us, with your permission, your name, email address and profile picture. We request only the basic openid, email and profile scopes. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google data.
  • Information you send us. For example, the content of an email you send to our support address.
  • Technical data. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To provide, maintain and improve the service.
  • To respond to your requests and send service-related messages.

We do not use your Google user data for advertising, and we never sell it.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

Aether’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that help us run the service, such as hosting, under confidentiality obligations;
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and others;
  • with your explicit consent.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing contact@example.com. We will delete it within 30 days.

You can also remove Aether’s access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted HTTPS connections, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

7. Children’s privacy

Aether is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at contact@example.com.
