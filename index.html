TASKFLOW

Desktop app for macOS

Every task,
next to its code.

TaskFlow keeps your task folders next to the repos they belong to, and turns client Q&A from Google Sheets into Markdown files your team and AI agents can read.

  • Find any task with ⌘K and open it in Finder or the built-in terminal
  • Read-only Google Sheets access to load the Q&A rows of each task
  • View and edit task docs without leaving the app
